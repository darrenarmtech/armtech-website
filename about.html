About ARMTECH

Precision Instruments. Honest Expertise. Real Support.

ARMTECH is a specialized distributor of test and measurement equipment, serving industrial professionals and educational institutions worldwide. We supply the instruments that keep workplaces safe, production lines compliant, and engineering students hands-on — from multimeters and power analyzers to sound level meters and environmental testers.

The Brands We Stand Behind

We carry equipment from manufacturers that engineers actually trust: Fluke, Lutron, Tenmar, Unitest, Testo, and other precision-grade brands. These are not commodity gadgets. They are the tools that safety officers rely on for compliance audits, that maintenance teams use to prevent costly downtime, and that lecturers place in students' hands so the next generation learns on real, professional-grade hardware.

Why We Exist

ARMTECH was founded after more than ten years in this industry — spent watching distributors treat test equipment as boxes to ship rather than solutions to match. This company was built to be the supplier that should have always existed: one where the person answering the phone can tell you whether a Fluke 87V or a Unitest series better suits your arc-flash application, or which Testo probe is right for a student's first thermodynamics project.
