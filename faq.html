Calibration Services

How often should I calibrate my instruments?

+

Calibration frequency depends on several factors:

  • Manufacturer recommendations: Typically 12 months for most instruments
  • Usage intensity: Heavy use may require 6-month intervals
  • Criticality: ISO/GMP environments often require annual or semi-annual calibration
  • Drift history: Instruments that consistently pass may extend to 24 months
  • Regulatory requirements: Some industries mandate specific intervals

Our technical team can recommend appropriate intervals based on your application.

Are your calibrations traceable?

+

Yes, all our calibrations are traceable to national measurement standards. Calibration certificates include measurement uncertainty statements and traceability documentation.

What's the typical turnaround time for calibration?

+

Standard calibration service is 3-5 working days. We also offer express 1-2 day service for urgent requirements (additional charges apply). On-site calibration can typically be scheduled within 1 week.

Do you offer on-site calibration services?

+

Yes, we provide on-site calibration for instruments that cannot be removed from service or are too large to transport. Our field technicians bring calibration standards to your facility. Contact us to discuss on-site service availability for your instruments.

What if my instrument fails calibration?

+

If an instrument is out of tolerance, our calibration certificate will document the as-found condition. We can perform adjustment to bring it within specifications, or recommend repair if adjustment is insufficient. All work is documented with as-found and as-left data.
