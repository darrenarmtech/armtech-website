What's Included in Our Calibration Service?

  • Pre-calibration assessment: Visual inspection and functionality check
  • As-found data: Recording instrument condition before adjustment
  • Calibration procedure: Following manufacturer specifications or recognized standards
  • Adjustment (if required): Bringing instrument within specifications
  • As-left data: Final measurement results after calibration
  • Calibration certificate: Formal documentation with traceability and uncertainty
  • Calibration sticker: Due date label affixed to instrument
